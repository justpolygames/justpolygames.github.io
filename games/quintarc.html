Your magic, your combination

QUINTARC is an elemental spellcasting game built around a floating grimoire and five orbs. Play as a Sage and combine Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Arcane to invoke 126 different spells.

Explore the narrative campaign, experiment in Practice, defend together in Ward, or take your combinations into competitive arenas.

Combine. Invoke. Cast.

01 / Choose five elements

Keys 1–5 select Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Arcane. Each press replaces the next highlighted orb. Fill all five positions to form your recipe.

02 / Invoke with Q

The combination is unordered: four Fire and one Arcane makes the same spell in any order. Invocation converges the orbs and stores the spell.

03 / Build a three-spell kit

The newest spell enters E, pushing older spells into R and T. Reinvoking a stored spell promotes it without duplicating it or resetting its cooldown.

04 / Cast and adapt

Use E, R, or T to cast. Each spell has its own mana cost and cooldown. Stored spells remain available after casting and respawning.

Manage your mana

Passive mana recovers at 1 per second. Invoke five Arcane orbs for Arcane Ascension: hold its cast key to restore 40 mana per second before affinity. You move slowly, take more damage, and hits interrupt the channel.

Read the battlefield

Ground spells need visible, supported ground. Walls and summons need clear placement space. Healing fields help living allied players; they do not revive players or heal the Ward core. Cleansing removes current negative statuses, without granting lasting immunity.

Ways to play

Story / The Fivefold Oath

A five-chapter solo campaign. Explore elemental realms, uncover discoveries, face seal guardians, and follow the story of your mentor Ilyra.

Practice

Choose any of the five elemental arenas for free practice, or work through 13 guided lessons.

Ward / 1–3 players

Defend the core together in cooperative combat. Ward can also begin solo.

Arcane Clash / 3v3

Bring your spell combinations into team competition. Requires six ready players.

Convergence / 6 players

A free-for-all arena where every other Sage is an opponent. Requires six ready players.

Multiplayer includes local-network and configured online rooms. Players must use matching builds; internet play and platform validation are still being developed.

Five elements, five arenas

Fire

Ridges and canyon routes reward careful positioning.

Water

Channel crossings with shallow, traversable beds change your approach.

Earth

Garden courts and balconies create layered combat spaces.

Air

A five-island cross connects distinct fighting spaces.

Arcane

Staggered gates and offset terraces break up sight lines.

Arena affinity rewards matching spell elements, up to 25% for a pure matching spell. Contested runes restore health or mana, or grant temporary magic, movement, or invisibility benefits.

Story: The Fivefold Oath

Follow your mentor Ilyra across five elemental realms and discover why she broke the council’s shared seal system. Secure guarded discoveries, defeat each seal guardian, and find the passage onward.

Press F to examine landmarks and use cleared passages. H opens the journal. Retry resumes the last completed encounter with the starter loadout restored; campaign progress and guided lessons save separately.

  3. What the Water Kept / Water: Drowned Cantor.
  4. The Last Messenger / Air: Hollow Herald.
  5. The Fivefold Oath / Arcane: the Unbound Regent dragon.

The final encounter concludes with the rescue of Ilyra.

Mobs & bosses

Meet the seven base enemy types. Health and damage can vary by encounter, so this field guide focuses on attack patterns.

MELEE PURSUER

Bonebound

A close-range skeleton enemy. Create space or interrupt its approach with your spells.

FAST LUNGE

Dusk Bat

A small, fast creature with a lunging attack. Watch the windup and move before contact.

RANGED SPITTER

Blight Slime

A slower ranged enemy that fires a projectile. Reposition or use cover to break its line of sight.

HEAVY AREA SLAM

Ossuary Warden

A tougher enemy with a sweeping ground slam. Move clear of the warning area before impact.

AREA SLAM

Crypt Sentinel

A sentinel with the heavy slam routine. Its area attack punishes staying too close.

THREE-SHOT VOLLEY

Hollow Seer

A ranged caster that sends three projectiles in a spread. Use cover or dodge away from the volley.

FLYING BOSS

Ash Drake

A winged enemy with charged flame volleys. Its three-shot volleys widen to five below half health.

Reveal the named campaign guardians
  • Rootbound Warden — Earth · Heavy melee slam.
  • Cinder Marshal — Fire · Sentinel slam.
  • Drowned Cantor — Water · Ranged slime attacks.
  • Hollow Herald — Air · Melee pursuit.
  • Unbound Regent — Arcane · Flying dragon with charged volleys.

The campaign guardians have their own names and encounters; the earlier bosses share attack routines with the base enemy types.

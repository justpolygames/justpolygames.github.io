Your magic, your combination

QUINTARC is an elemental spellcasting game built around a floating grimoire and five orbs. Play as a Sage and combine Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Arcane to invoke 126 different spells.

Explore the narrative campaign, experiment in Practice, defend together in Ward, or take your combinations into competitive arenas.

Spells now have specific targeting, timing, movement, resource, and terrain interactions. Mire Guardian holds a fixed area; Magma Eruption opens a narrow ground fissure. The cards below describe each spell’s current behavior. Encapsulate enemies in ice with Flash Freeze and Blizzard. Matching Earth, Fire, electrical, and Arcane skills apply Poison, Burn, Shock, and Mana Drain. Shock prevents new invocations while leaving stored spells available. Shields block incoming debuffs; cleanse skills remove active effects.

Combine. Invoke. Cast.

01 / Choose five elements

Keys 1–5 select Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Arcane. Each press replaces the next highlighted orb. Fill all five positions to form your recipe.

02 / Invoke with Q

The combination is unordered: four Fire and one Arcane makes the same spell in any order. Invocation converges the orbs and stores the spell.

03 / Build a three-spell kit

The newest spell enters E, pushing older spells into R and T. Reinvoking a stored spell promotes it without duplicating it or resetting its cooldown.

04 / Cast and adapt

Use E, R, or T to cast. Each spell has its own mana cost and cooldown. Stored spells remain available after casting and respawning.

Manage your mana

Passive mana recovers at 1 per second. Invoke five Arcane orbs for Arcane Ascension: hold its cast key to restore 40 mana per second before affinity. You move slowly, take more damage, and hits interrupt the channel.

Read the battlefield

Ground spells need visible, supported ground. Walls and summons need clear placement space. Healing fields help living allied players; they do not revive players or heal the Ward core. Cleansing removes current negative statuses, without granting lasting immunity.

New in 0.1.1

Choose one of five complete wizard looks—Ember, Tide, Grove, Gale, or Astral—and customize your skin tone with four swatches.

Spell effects now have more distinct motion: rising steam, falling rain, growing ice shards, swirling sand, and gravity wells. Eight beam spells have their own visual treatments. Effects and short spell sounds prepare during loading to reduce work when casting; performance improvements are ongoing.

42 matching offensive spells now apply timed elemental debuffs. The grimoire below includes their current effects, durations, and damage or mana rates. Multiplayer players must use matching builds.

Elemental debuffs

Freeze / Ice

Flash Freeze and Blizzard encase enemies in ice, immobilize them, interrupt casting, and deal damage over time. A stored cleanse remains usable.

Poison / Earth

Selected Earth-related skills deal damage each second for a short duration.

Burn / Fire

Selected Fire-related skills leave damage that continues after the initial hit.

Shock / Electrical

Deals damage over time and blocks new invocations. Already invoked spells remain available to cast.

Mana Drain / Arcane

Drains mana over time instead of health. It does not transfer mana to the caster.

All five expire automatically. Active shields block new debuffs, including on a hit that breaks the shield. Cleansing removes active debuffs immediately. Repeated applications refresh an effect rather than stacking copies; different effects can coexist. Standing in a hostile field can apply them again after cleansing.

Elemental counters & resistance

The arena matters: Fire ↔ Water and Earth ↔ Air counter each other. Arcane has no opposing element. Matching orbs strengthen your spell; orbs opposed by the arena weaken it. The effect is weighted by the five orbs in the recipe.

ArenaMatching elementCountered element
Ember CalderaFireWater
Tidal SanctuaryWaterFire
Verdant RuinsEarthAir
Zephyr IslesAirEarth
Astral ParadoxArcaneNone

Five matching orbs give +25% potency; five countered orbs give −20%. For example, 3 Fire + 2 Water in a Fire arena gives +15% −8% = +7%. Other elements are neutral. Damage, recovery, shield strength and numerical buff bonuses scale with potency. Spell range, movement distance and ordinary status duration do not scale with map affinity.

Know your opponent

Enemy resistance is separate from the arena and scales by the elements in the spell. A pure Earth attack deals 35% less damage to an Ossuary Warden; a pure Air attack deals 20% more. Mixed recipes apply only the corresponding fraction of each modifier. Arena potency and enemy resistance multiply.

Control resistance affects Stun, Freeze, Root, Silence and Slow. It also reduces Slow’s movement penalty. Special status modifiers multiply with control resistance when both apply. Poison, Burn, Freeze and Shock damage ticks also respect elemental damage resistance.

Sturdy enemies with control resistance develop temporary resistance to repeated Stun, Freeze and Root: each successful application halves the next duration, down to one eighth of the already-resistant duration. Four seconds without another application resets this. A refresh cannot shorten a stronger control effect that is already active.

Campaign bosses

Campaign bosses resist their chapter’s element by 35% (the Fire boss: 45%) and take 20% more from its opposing element. The Arcane boss has no elemental weakness. Boss control durations are 65% shorter, and push/pull/throw distance is 20% of normal. Their creature’s special status resistance remains: the dragon cannot Burn, and Freeze duration is further halved. Resistance does not make every boss immune to control.

Waves, shields & counterplay

Friendly waves pass through one another. Opposing wave fronts clash: strengths within 10% cancel both waves; otherwise the stronger wave continues with the weaker wave’s damage subtracted and proportionally less knockback. This rule applies to wave skills, not every pair of spell effects. Waves push eligible enemies, with walls and resistance limiting displacement.

Terra Nexus creates a visible dome with 180 base health, a 6m radius and a 5-second lifetime. It blocks magic crossing the boundary in either direction, for either team. Allies shelter inside, but cannot fire through it. Enemies who enter are protected from outside spells too. Players may walk through; spells cast and resolved entirely inside still work. Attacks can break the dome, including allied attacks.

Personal shields protect only their wearer from all directions and use a close-fitting shell. Construct walls cover the directions their geometry actually blocks; opponents can attack around their sides or behind them. Steam Quake now erupts at the caster’s feet and strikes moving enemies nearby.

Active buffs and debuffs appear at the bottom left as circular icons with remaining seconds and draining timer rings. Choose your character and available cosmetic accessories in Your Sage.
